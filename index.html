About Me

I am a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at the Technological University of the Philippines Manila. I enjoy exploring diverse areas of software engineering, including responsive web design, robust backends, IoT, and modern networking.

For me, learning becomes meaningful when I can put concepts into practice. I learn best through hands-on experimentation, debugging real-world problems, and refining systems through methodical trial and improvement.

I also value collaborating with other developers. Working in teams provides opportunities to learn diverse viewpoints, grasp complex architectures faster, and build lasting professional connections. Outside of software development, I like tinkering with hardware and wiring, joining tech community events, and supporting fellow students in their tech journey.
